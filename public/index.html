Accuracy

A phone compass reads magnetic north. Across Southern Africa magnetic north sits roughly 17° to 28° west of true north, so the app corrects every reading with the World Magnetic Model (WMM 2025) for your position. If the arrow wanders, move the phone in a figure‑8 a few times and keep it away from metal, magnets and speakers. On iPhone the compass must be allowed each time the page is opened.

If your phone has no compass, the app still gives the true bearing and the magnetic bearing to set on a hand compass. Bearings are great‑circle bearings from your position; distances are in kilometres.
